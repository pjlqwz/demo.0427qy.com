As a factory that has obtained the national medical device manufacturing enterprise license, our company designs and produces products in strict accordance with the international ISO13485 quality system standards and CE certification requirements certified by the NQA agency. Customers are located in North America, the European Union, Japan, South Korea, Southeast Asia, Australia and New Zealand, Central Africa and other regions and countries, and are well received by customers and encouraged and supported by the municipal government. All employees of the company have always adhered to the tenet of "customer first, quality first, service first" and the professional style of "professionalism, concentration and focus". We sincerely welcome customers at home and abroad to call us. The company is willing to cooperate with customers at home and abroad. Cooperation, go hand in hand to achieve a win-win situation.

 

The main products of our company:

1. Non-woven products: disposable non-woven masks, three-dimensional anti-haze masks, PM2.5 masks, printed masks, lab coats, work clothes, shoe covers, boot covers, round caps, grinding hats, work caps, pillowcases , Sheets, bedspreads, etc.

2. Plastic products: Breathable membrane boot covers, CPE shoe covers, PE shoe covers, PE cuffs, breathable membrane cuffs, PE aprons, etc.

3. Disposable leisure beauty products: briefs, shorts, thongs, slippers, robes, sauna clothes, etc.
